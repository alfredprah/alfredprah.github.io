How I work

I optimize for shipping. The products on this site went from idea to live store in weeks, not quarters, because that's the only way to know if something is real. The same instinct shows up in my enterprise work: I'd rather prototype against a customer's real data on day one than spend a month producing a deck about it.

I also write — about the architecture, psychology, and product decisions behind what I ship. The most recent piece is on why Momenta works: the research and design thinking behind a wellness app built specifically for mothers, not wellness enthusiasts.
